Last updated: September 26, 2026.

workspace-mcp-personal is operated by Jiahao Yu for his own email and calendar workflows. This policy describes how the integration handles Google account data.

### Data access and use

With the account owner's authorization, the integration accesses basic Google account identity information; Gmail messages, metadata, attachments, drafts, labels, and supported mailbox settings; and Google Calendar calendars, events, descriptions, and attendee information. The granted permissions determine which operations are available.

Data is used to carry out the owner's requested email and calendar tasks, including searching, summarizing, drafting, sending, organizing messages, and managing events. Google handles sign-in. The integration receives OAuth tokens and does not request or store the owner's Google password.

### Processing and sharing

The MCP server runs on computers or servers selected by the owner. Information returned to an AI assistant may be transmitted to the assistant's model provider and retained in its conversation history or logs. For this setup, selected clients may include Codex, Grok Bot, and Cursor. The owner controls which clients and providers are connected; their processing and retention depend on the selected service and account settings.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Transfers to connected services are limited to providing the email and calendar features requested by the owner. Connected services must be used with settings and terms compatible with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address security incidents.

### Storage, retention, and deletion

OAuth credentials are stored in the configured credential storage on the owner's computers or servers. Task outputs, downloaded attachments, and diagnostic logs may also remain on those systems or in connected clients. Retention follows the owner's configuration and the connected services' retention settings; there is no single automatic deletion period across all clients.

The owner can stop future access by removing the app in [Google Account third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revocation does not erase previously saved messages, files, logs, or conversations. Those copies must be deleted from the relevant device or service using its available controls. The public information pages do not contain OAuth tokens or mailbox content.

### Contact and changes

For questions or deletion requests, contact [jiahaoyu04@gmail.com](mailto:jiahaoyu04@gmail.com). Material changes to data use will be disclosed here, and renewed consent will be obtained when required before using Google data for a new purpose.
