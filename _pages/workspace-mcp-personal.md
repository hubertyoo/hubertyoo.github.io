workspace-mcp-personal is a private email and calendar integration operated by Jiahao Yu for his own Google account. It connects Gmail and Google Calendar to AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

The integration supports finding and summarizing email, preparing and sending messages, organizing mailbox labels, and reading or managing calendars and events. Actions are performed according to the account owner's instructions and the permissions granted through Google's sign-in and consent screens.

Email and calendar information returned by the integration can be processed by the AI assistant and model provider selected by the account owner. This website provides information about the integration; it does not provide a public sign-up service or collect Google passwords.
